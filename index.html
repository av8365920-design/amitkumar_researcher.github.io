01 / About

Engineering ideas into reliable networks.

I am an Electronics & Communication Engineering researcher and educator with a focus on 5G vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication, wireless networks, resource allocation and network performance.

My Ph.D. thesis, submitted at the National Institute of Technology Surat, focuses on Resource Allocation in 5G V2X Communication. My work combines communication theory, optimization, simulation and intelligent networking approaches.
